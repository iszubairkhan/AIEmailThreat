    How to Enable 24/7 Background Monitoring

    1. Click 24/7 Monitoring Setup at the top of this page.
    2. Enter your preferred Incident Alert Email and click Copy Script Code.
    3. Open script.google.com and click New Project.
    4. Paste the copied script and press Save (Ctrl+S).
    5. Click Triggers (Clock icon) → + Add Trigger → select autoScanRecentEmails → Minutes timer (Every 1 minute).

    How to Disconnect or Disable Monitoring

    To Disconnect Web Session: Click the Disconnect Mailbox button on the triage hub to immediately terminate OAuth tokens.

    To Stop 24/7 Apps Script: Go to script.google.com → click Triggers → click the three dots on the trigger → select Delete trigger.

    To Revoke Third-Party Permission: Visit myaccount.google.com/connections → find AI Threat Sentinel → click Delete all connections.
