    Sentinel Help Desk & Quick Guide

    Automated Background Monitoring

    1. Click 1-Click Connect Any Mailbox to link your Gmail account.
    2. Your inbox is audited 24/7 in the cloud — you can safely close this browser window.
    3. Incoming unread messages are checked automatically by the configured background monitor.
    4. High-risk incidents (≥ 40%) generate SHA-256 evidence records and dispatch email alerts.

    Disconnecting & Revoking Access

    1. Stop Background Daemon: Click Disconnect on the triage bar above to halt server monitoring and wipe active tokens instantly.

    2. Clear Mailbox Tags: Click Clear Labels to strip all SOC-SCANNED tags from your Gmail inbox.

    3. Permanent Google Revocation: To permanently revoke Sentinel's OAuth access, visit Google Connections → select AI Email Threat Sentinel → Delete all connections.
